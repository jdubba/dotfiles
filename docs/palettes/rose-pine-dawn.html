rose-pine-dawn

The light palette as shipped in themes/rose-pine-dawn/, plus the seams derived from it. Slot names (c0–c15, bg, fg) are the vocabulary for talking about a theme — use them rather than hex. Text pairs are WCAG: 4.5:1 for text, 3.0:1 for chrome.

Beyond the ANSI slots

Roles this theme's upstream spec defines that the sixteen ANSI slots cannot carry. Usable in waybar, walker, hypr/hyprlock, starship and tmux — GTK CSS, raw hex and truecolor escapes take any colour. Not usable in kitty/ghostty, whose palette is the sixteen, nor in bat/fzf, which follow it. Using one means hand-maintaining that seam: the emitter derives only from the ANSI slots.

Starship powerline separators

A separator is a filled glyph — one pill's background drawn on the next pill's — so it is judged by perceptual distance (CIELAB ΔE), not WCAG. WCAG measures luminance only and rates plainly visible pairs as failures: amber on plum is 1.39:1 and unmistakable at 79 ΔE. Under 12 ΔE the two pills genuinely merge.
