Starship powerline separators

A separator is a filled glyph — one pill's background drawn on the next pill's — so it is judged by perceptual distance (CIELAB ΔE), not WCAG. WCAG measures luminance only and rates plainly visible pairs as failures: amber on plum is 1.39:1 and unmistakable at 79 ΔE. Under 12 ΔE the two pills genuinely merge.
